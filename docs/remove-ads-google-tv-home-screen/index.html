Remove the ads from your Google TV home screen

Google TV fills its home screen with sponsored rows, promoted apps and a big advert at the top. There's no setting to turn them off, but you can replace the home screen itself. No root, no computer needed.

Why the ads are there

The stock Google TV launcher is built to recommend things, and some of those recommendations are paid for. The banner at the top, the "Sponsored" rows and the apps it suggests installing are all part of the home screen app itself, so blocking a few domains or turning off "personalised recommendations" only changes which adverts you see, not whether you see them.

The fix is to change the home screen app. On Android TV that's called a launcher, and you can install a different one.

Step 1: install an ad-free launcher

Flix Launcher is a free, open-source Android TV launcher with no ads, no sponsored rows and no analytics. It shows your apps, the episode you were halfway through and films worth starting, and nothing else.

  1. On the TV, install Downloader from the Play Store.
  2. Open it and enter https://github.com/HatriGt/flix-launcher/releases/latest/download/flix-launcher.apk.
  3. Install the APK when it finishes downloading. If asked, allow Downloader to install unknown apps.

Step 2: make the Home button open it

Google TV always sends the Home button to its own launcher, even if you pick a different default. Flix gets around this with a tiny accessibility service that only listens for the Home key.

  1. Open Settings → System → Accessibility (on some TVs, Settings → Accessibility).
  2. Choose Flix Launcher and switch it on.
  3. Press Home. Flix opens, without an advert in sight.

Optional: if you have a computer with adb, run this once so Flix can switch the service back on by itself after updates.

adb shell pm grant com.flix.launcher android.permission.WRITE_SECURE_SETTINGS

What you get instead

  • Your favourite apps on top, in the order you choose.
  • Continue watching and film rows from Stremio-compatible addons, if you use Nuvio.
  • Aerial video or photo backgrounds instead of adverts.
  • A home screen that loads fast even on 2 GB TVs: the whole app is about 700 KB.

Questions

Can I turn off ads in Google TV settings?

No. Apps only mode removes recommendations, but it also removes the rows you want, and the top banner can still be promoted content. Replacing the launcher is the only way to get a clean home screen.

Do I need to root my TV?

No. Flix Launcher uses an accessibility service that listens only for the Home key, which works on stock, unrooted Google TV and Android TV.

Can I go back to the stock home screen?

Yes. Switch Flix off in Settings → Accessibility, or uninstall it. Leave the stock Google TV home installed; Flix never removes it.
