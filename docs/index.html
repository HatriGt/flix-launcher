No ads. No tracking.

No sponsored rows and no analytics. Flix only talks to the services it shows: your addons, Nuvio if you sign in, the weather and the background videos.

Home means home

Google TV keeps the Home button for itself. Flix takes it back with a tiny accessibility service that only listens for that key, and can switch it back on by itself after updates.

Light on cheap TVs

About 54 MB of memory on a 2 GB TV. Icons are drawn once, and artwork loads only for the card you're looking at.

Yours to shape

Four themes, icon shapes and sizes, rows you pick and order, poster sizes and backgrounds. Every setting is searchable.

Updates itself

New versions download in the background. An "Update ready" pill appears on the home screen; press OK, or let it install while the TV is idle.

On your TV
in three steps.

Flix isn't on the Play Store, so you install it once by hand. After that it updates itself.

1

Get the APK

Download flix-launcher.apk (about 700 KB), or pick a version from the releases.

2

Install it on the TV

Use a sideloading app like Downloader or Send Files to TV, or from a computer: adb install flix-launcher.apk

3

Make it home

Open Flix and follow the setup. In Settings → Accessibility, switch on Flix Launcher, and the Home button opens Flix.

Questions

How do I remove ads from the Google TV home screen?

Install Flix Launcher and switch it on in Settings → Accessibility. The Home button then opens Flix, which has no ads or sponsored rows. The stock launcher stays installed, unused.

Which TVs does it run on?

Any Android TV or Google TV device on Android 9 or later, including Sony BRAVIA, TCL, Hisense, Philips, Xiaomi Mi Box, Chromecast with Google TV and NVIDIA Shield.

Does it work with Nuvio and Stremio addons?

Yes. Sign in with Nuvio for Continue watching and your addons' rows. Titles open in Nuvio, including the Play Store version and the NuvioPlusIPTV fork.

Is it free?

Yes, free and open source under the MIT licence, with no ads and no analytics.

How does it update?

New versions download in the background from GitHub. An "Update ready" pill appears on the home screen; it installs when the TV is idle or when you press OK.

How do I go back to the stock launcher?

Switch off Flix Launcher in Settings → Accessibility, or uninstall it.
